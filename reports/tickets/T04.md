# T04: Sessions, expiry conventions and identity time helpers

Status: **complete**. Date: 2026-10-25.

## Files

`src/marketagg/calendar/{__init__.py,registry.py,sessions.py}`; `tests/unit/test_calendar.py` (13 tests).

## Behaviour

- Built on `exchange_calendars` XNYS with explicit left-labelled minutes.
- Session sizes:
  - normal sessions have 390 minutes, with session-4h buckets of 240 and 150;
  - the 2025-11-28 early close has 210 minutes and one 210-minute 4h bucket;
  - the March DST transition moves the UTC open;
  - holidays and weekends have no session and no phantom minutes.
- A synthetic exceptional closure is supported.
- DTE counts calendar days. The exercise cutoff is never guessed. Product overrides are metadata only.
- Dates outside the calendar's range are refused.
- `filterwarnings=error` exposed a third-party deprecation warning (exchange_calendars on pandas 3). It is handled so the suite stays warning-clean.

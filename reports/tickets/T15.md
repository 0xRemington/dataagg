## Behaviour

- **Reproducibility:** the same pinned request reproduces the semantic hash, and a later correction never changes an old export.
- **Summaries:** missing data, gaps and invalidation lineage are summarised in the manifest.
- **Output files:** CSV and Parquet, plus `lineage.parquet`.
- **Job safety:** failed and cancelled jobs publish nothing (staging plus an atomic rename). Restart recovery requeues jobs and cleans staging.
- **Security:** CSV formula cells are neutralised. Signed download links expire and reject tampering.

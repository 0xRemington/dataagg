# T09: Deterministic aggregation and correction propagation

Status: **complete**. Date: 2026-10-25.

## Files

`src/marketagg/analytics/aggregate.py`; `tests/unit/test_aggregate.py` (9 tests), `tests/integration/test_reaggregation.py` (5 tests).

## Behaviour

- **Bucket counts:** a full normal session yields 78 × 5m, 26 × 15m and session-4h buckets of 240 and 150 minutes. An early close yields one 210-minute 4h bucket.
- **Completeness:** a bucket with a missing minute is incomplete. Research excludes it by default, and display can include it with explicit partial flags.
- **Publication timing:** a bucket is never published before its interval ends.
- **Corrections:** a late correction creates a new version, and the old asof view is unchanged.
- **Lineage:** every input minute is referenced. Duplicate minutes are rejected, null volume propagates, and daily reconciliation reports differences without overwriting.

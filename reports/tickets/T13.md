## Behaviour

- **Auth:** separate bearer-token scopes for read and export, compared in constant time. Errors use one envelope. Health responses contain no secrets, and readiness checks the database, migration head and partitions.
- **Serialisation:** decimals are strings and times are UTC `Z`.
- **Queries:** cursor pagination stays stable while ingestion continues. Page sizes and ranges are bounded, with concurrency capped by a semaphore.
- **Errors:** an incompatible basis returns 409.
- **Option history:** keeps nulls and unknown freshness.
- **Export lifecycle:** requires the export scope. The test client needs the `httpx2` dev dependency.

# T12: Descriptive quote metrics and simple term/skew panels

Status: **complete**. Date: 2026-10-25.

## Files

`src/marketagg/analytics/quote_metrics.py`; `tests/unit/test_quote_metrics.py` (8 tests).

## Behaviour

- **Midpoints:** none for zero, crossed or negative quotes, or a zero denominator.
- **Spreads:** wide spreads are reported, not hidden.
- **IV:** impossible values are excluded. Outputs are labelled as temporally uncertain when the quote time is unknown.
- **ATM:** ties go to the lower strike.
- **Term structure:** absent expiries are listed, and a mismatched spot basis is rejected.
- **Skew:** the near-ATM definition is bounded by cycle skew.
- **Cumulative fields:** volume and OI are never summed.
- No Greeks are produced.

# T08: Rolling minutes and gap recovery

Status: **complete**. Date: 2026-10-25.

## Files

`src/marketagg/collectors/{bars.py,recovery.py}`; `tests/integration/test_minutes.py` (8 tests).

## Behaviour

- **What is persisted:** only closed, in-session minutes, after a safe lag. Overlapping refetches and disordered rows are idempotent.
- **Revisions:** a revised bar appends a revision and marks the containing derived buckets dirty. Invalidation hides a minute without reviving its predecessor.
- **Gaps:** missing minutes are detected and never synthesized.
  - A gap is repaired by a sweep while the source still has the data.
  - Beyond the source horizon it is marked unrecoverable.
  - A halt needs authoritative evidence.
  - Weekend scans create no gaps.

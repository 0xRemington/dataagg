# T07: Daily backfill, actions and reconciliation basis

Status: **complete**. Date: 2026-10-25.

## Files

`src/marketagg/collectors/bars.py` (the daily history and corporate actions section); `tests/integration/test_daily.py` (6 tests).

**Path deviation:** actions ingestion (`ingest_actions`) lives in `collectors/bars.py` instead of a separate `collectors/actions.py`. Daily backfill and actions share chunking and resume code. Behaviour and tests are unaffected.

## Behaviour

- **Chunking:** annual chunks are half-open with overlap. A durable resume cursor survives crashes.
- **Revisions:** unchanged refetched bars are not duplicated, and changed values append revisions.
- **Holidays:** the fake provider returns daily bars on holidays. The collector drops non-session days and counts them, rather than storing phantom session bars.
- **Corporate actions:** stored bitemporally. A split known later flags incompatible price bases.
- **Backfill visibility:** backfilled history is never visible to observed-mode decisions made before it was fetched.
- **Empty history:** an empty response does not imply delisting.

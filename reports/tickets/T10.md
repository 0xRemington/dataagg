## Behaviour

- **Expiry selection:** distinct expiries nearest the 30/45/60/90 DTE targets inside the band. Ties go to the earlier expiry. A sparse band selects fewer expiries rather than leaving the band.
- **Request cost:** each priority slot's cost is asserted exactly.
- **Polls:** two equal-price polls are two observations, and a replayed response is one.
- **Partial responses:** a partial response keeps the valid side with a coverage flag. Bad and ambiguous rows are quarantined to `quality_issues` while the other rows are kept.
- **Nulls and flags:** stored as observed.
- **Discovery timing:** if no discovery was known before a slot, the slot is skipped with a gap. Expiry rolls append membership changes.

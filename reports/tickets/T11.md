## Behaviour

Every release-blocking temporal adversary in doc 05 has a test:

- delayed publication, with exact `<=` and one-microsecond boundaries;
- later correction and later invalidation, with no fallback;
- delayed option commit;
- quote age unknown, never execution-eligible;
- no Friday-to-Monday carry;
- late derived publication, observed vs recomputed;
- universe and terms leakage;
- mixed sources rejected, and basis mismatch returns 409;
- backfill excluded from observed history.

Three **mutation tests** prove the predicates are load-bearing. Removing the knowledge predicate, selecting latest-first, or removing the closed-bar predicate each makes an adversary leak. The SQL predicates are module constants so the tests can swap them.

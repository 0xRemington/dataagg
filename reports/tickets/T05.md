## Behaviour

- **Job identity and leases:** job keys are deterministic, so two schedulers produce one logical job set. An advisory lock elects a single planner. Claims carry a fencing `lease_token`, and a stale worker cannot complete or commit.
- **Overdue slots:** a live option slot past its deadline is skipped and recorded as an unrecoverable `missing_chain_snapshot` gap. It is never refetched as history.
- **Pacing and errors:**
  - pacing is shared across workers;
  - a 429 opens a provider-wide circuit that honours Retry-After;
  - 401/403 disables the provider and opens an incident;
  - retry delays use bounded full jitter within the job deadline.
- **Budgets:** the planner reproduces the guide's worked request-budget arithmetic and explains infeasible budgets. Early closes move close-relative jobs. Under backlog, broad and hourly scopes are shed first, each recorded as a skipped job plus a gap.
- **Worker:** records actual endpoint and transport requests per run. Payloads are spooled atomically.

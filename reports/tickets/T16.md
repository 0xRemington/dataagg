# T16: Archives and unified hot/cold reads

Status: **complete**. Date: 2026-10-25.

## Files

`src/marketagg/storage/{archive.py,payloads.py}`; `tests/integration/test_archive.py` (6 tests).

## Behaviour

- **Archive steps:** write a temporary object, verify count/type/hash, copy and check off-host, then switch the catalog atomically.
- **Round trip:** preserves types, identities, Decimal, nulls, UTC and nanoseconds.
- **Reads:** hot, mixed and cold reads agree with no duplicates.
- **Corrections:** a late correction creates a new generation, and old pins are unchanged.
- **Failures:** missing or corrupt objects fail visibly. An interrupted archive loses nothing.
- **Pruning:** requires off-site verification, the 7-day grace period and restore proof. On 2026-10-26 it also gained an explicit `DATASETS` allowlist check, matching `archive_month`.
